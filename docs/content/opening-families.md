# Opening family selection

Scan64 currently curates three opening families for its interactive explorer:

| Family | Side | Instructional focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Italian Game | White after `1. e4 e5` | Development, central control, and kingside coordination. |
| Queen’s Gambit | White after `1. d4 d5` | Central tension, pawn structure, and queenside development. |
| Caro-Kann Defense | Black after `1. e4 c6` | Resilient central structure and counterplay. |

These families are intentionally limited. They provide a focused first content set for the Opening Explorer rather than claiming complete opening coverage.

## Deferred families

- Tactical gambits, including the King’s Gambit.
- Closed or flank systems, including the English Opening.
- Black repertoires against `1. d4`, including Indian systems.

Opening Explorer missions are recorded separately from player-owned diagnosed lessons. They do not advance the profile and review path used by Daily Training.
