Scan64 bundled chess piece artwork
=================================
The SVG artwork in these directories is separately licensed. It is NOT relicensed
under Scan64's GPL-3.0-or-later software licence. The presence of the three
non-commercial sets means the complete bundled artwork is NOT offered for
unrestricted commercial use. Selecting another set does not remove these
restrictions from redistribution of the bundled non-commercial artwork.

The 108 SVGs in every directory except sophia/ are distributed unmodified from
lichess-org/lila/public/piece. The cburnett, merida and mpchess SVGs were taken
at lila commit 47948db658a1678d14e97d41c1bcf82958e70ded. The 12 sophia/ SVGs
are distributed unmodified from GNOME Chess (see sophia/ below).
